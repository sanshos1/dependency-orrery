# Design identity — Dependency Orrery

The page is an instrument, not a dashboard. A single circular star field occupies the viewport. Sources enter as three orbiting bodies; resolving draws one continuous dependency path into the centre. Editing happens in a narrow astronomical index that rotates in from the right. There are no card grids, no bottom input bar, and no repeated form panels.

Signature interaction: drag the brass sight around the orbit to inspect one source, then press the central nucleus to resolve. Motion and radial geometry communicate reachability.
